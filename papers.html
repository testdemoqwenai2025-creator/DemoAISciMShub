How concept-card matching works

Every concept card declares keywords (in src/lib/data/concepts.ts). The /api/papers endpoint fetches arXiv RSS, parses each paper's title + abstract, and matches against the union of all concept card keywords. Papers matching ≥1 keyword are surfaced with the matched concept card IDs as deep-links. This is what makes the concept cards "living documents" — they reference the latest research, not just historical papers.

Step 1

Server fetches arXiv RSS

Once per fetch, 5 categories, ~25 papers each = ~125 total papers. Cached for 5 minutes on the server.

Step 2

Keyword match against concepts

Each paper's title + abstract is matched against 7 concept cards' keyword union (~50 keywords). Substring match on lowercased text.

Step 3

Deep-link to concept card

Papers that match ≥1 concept card are surfaced with the matched concept IDs as clickable deep-links to the card popup.
