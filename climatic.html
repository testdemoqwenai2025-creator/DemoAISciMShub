Sub-section 1

Atmospheric Circulation

1 card

ERA5 · jet streams · atmospheric rivers · blocking

Atmospheric circulation pattern detection from ERA5 reanalysis: jet streams, atmospheric blocking, atmospheric rivers (AR). Replaces hand-tuned IVT thresholds with learned mass-consistent objective detection. The OSIRIS clamp c=10 m/s matches wind field uncertainty.

Sub-section 2

Ocean State

1 card

SST · AMOC · ENSO · Argo floats

Ocean state reconstruction from multi-sensor SST (NOAA OISST, Sentinel-3, GHRSST) fused with Argo float profiles. Mixed-layer depth, AMOC strength proxies, and ENSO indices with full Bayesian uncertainty propagation. The OSIRIS clamp c=0.25 K matches SST analysis uncertainty.

Sub-section 3

Sea Ice & Cryosphere

1 card

CryoSat-2 · ICESat-2 · freeboard · thickness

Arctic + Antarctic sea ice thickness reconstruction from radar (CryoSat-2) and laser (ICESat-2) freeboard with explicit snow-depth coupling. Respects Archimedes buoyancy — prevents the unphysical negative thickness tails of unconstrained CNNs. The OSIRIS clamp c=0.04 m matches freeboard uncertainty.

Sub-section 4

Climate Projections

1 card

CMIP6 · SSP scenarios · downscaling · extremes

Statistical + neural downscaling of CMIP6 GCM projections to 5 km regional fields, with conservation of long-term mean + quantiles (avoiding distributional drift). Outputs extremes indices (TXx, R95pTOT) for adaptation decisions. The OSIRIS clamp c=0.5 K matches downscaling uncertainty.
