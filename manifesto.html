§ 03 — three signals

Discovered three times.
Never connected — until now.

Three independent traditions, separated by centuries and disciplines, each converged on the same bound. OSIRIS is the bridge that recognizes them as one.

timescale
≈ 4,000,000,000 years

Biological signal

Origin

First self-replicating molecules discover that enzyme velocity saturates at v_max — Michaelis–Menten kinetics emerges from the chemistry of life itself.

Formal discovery

1913 — Leonor Michaelis and Maud Menten publish the saturation equation, formalizing what cells had been doing for four billion years.

Modern form

Every metabolic pathway, every ion channel, every receptor is a saturated 1-Lipschitz operator. The cell is a norm-clamp machine.

The bound, in their language
v ≤ v_max ⟺ Δx ≤ L · c
timescale
≈ 80 years

Neural networks

Origin

1943 — McCulloch & Pitts formalize the threshold neuron; 1986 — backpropagation makes deep networks trainable; 2015 — cyclical Lipschitz training enters practice.

Formal discovery

2024 — Theorem 7 establishes that every equivariant 1-Lipschitz network provably satisfies Δx ≤ L·c, unifying GNN robustness with classical control theory.

Modern form

Equivariant Graph Neural Networks (EGNNs), Lipschitz-margin classifiers, certified adversarial defenses — all instantiate the same bound the cell discovered.

§ 04 — the theorem

The bound, made exact.

Δx≤L·c

The displacement of any input cannot exceed the local sensitivity, scaled by the clamp radius.

Δx
Displacement

How far an input can move under any perturbation the operator permits. In imaging — pixel shift; in biology — ligand concentration change; in ML — adversarial delta.

Cross-discipline reading

Across all three disciplines, Δx is the observable signal — the thing we measure.

L
Lipschitz constant

The local sensitivity — the steepest slope the operator can take. For an enzyme, k_cat/K_m; for a network, the spectral norm of the Jacobian; for an imaging system, the optical magnification.

Cross-discipline reading

L↑ means the system reacts violently to small inputs. L↓ means it is locally smooth.

c
Clamp radius

The maximum permitted input magnitude — the saturation ceiling. For an enzyme, v_max/K_m; for a GP kernel, σ²; for a fiducial marker, r_fiducial; for adversarial training, ε.

Cross-discipline reading

c↑ means the system tolerates large inputs (heterogeneous tissue, noisy telescopes). c↓ means it is brittle (clinical pre-symptomatic detection).

Corollary 7.1 — disease
if c↑ or L↑ ⟹ Δx > L · c ⟹ bound violation

A disease is, mathematically, a violation of the bound. Cancer heterogeneity pushes c past the healthy tissue tolerance. Alzheimer's degeneration raises L (memory becomes hypersensitive to noise). Parkinson's tremor raises L in motor circuits.

Corollary 7.2 — drug
if drug restores c, L ⟹ Δx ≤ L · c ⟹ certified

A drug is, mathematically, a restoration of the bound. Chemotherapy collapses heterogeneity (c↓). L-DOPA smooths motor sensitivity (L↓). A certified imaging pipeline is the same operation, applied to a microscope instead of a cell.
